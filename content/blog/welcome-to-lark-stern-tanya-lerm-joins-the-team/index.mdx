##### Written by: Frieda van der Merwe and Tanya Lerm

We’re excited to introduce the newest member of the Lark & Stern family — **Tanya Lerm**, who has joined us as our **Administrative Assistant**!

Tanya brings a wonderful mix of heart, skill, and dedication to our team. With a background in administrative and research work — as well as time spent as an Au Pair — she has built strong organisational and communication skills that are already making a difference behind the scenes.

Here’s a little more about Tanya:

✨ **She’s 27 and has lived in Pretoria her whole life** — a true local with a love for her community.

🍰 **A passionate home chef and baker**, Tanya finds joy in the kitchen and occasionally unwinds with a paintbrush in hand.

💛 **Her heart is deeply rooted in her faith and in service**, with a strong commitment to uplifting others and supporting her church and community.

We’re thrilled to have Tanya’s warm spirit and capable hands as part of our journey. We know she’s going to play a key role in keeping things running smoothly at Lark & Stern.

**Welcome to the team, Tanya!** 🎉
