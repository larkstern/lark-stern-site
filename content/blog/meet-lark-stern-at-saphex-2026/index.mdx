We are excited to invite you to join us at Saphex 2026 on **25–26 March 2026** at the Sandton Convention Centre.

At Lark & Stern Core Services, we specialize in delivering efficient, end-to-end solutions tailored to the unique needs of the Life Sciences and Pharmaceutical industries. Our expertise spans SAP integration, configuration, deployment, and infrastructure, enabling seamless system connectivity and optimized operational performance across manufacturing environments.

In collaboration with Pangaea Life Science Solutions, we are focused on enhancing enterprise performance through innovative digital platforms, including:

• Digital Thread for Life Sciences (DTLS)

• Simplified Manufacturing Process Layer (SiMPL)

• Batch Disposition Cockpit (BDC)

These solutions are designed to improve operational visibility, streamline manufacturing processes, and accelerate batch release—helping organizations achieve measurable digital outcomes across their value chain.

We are excited to launch our Latte Lab, an innovative live demonstration that showcases shopfloor integration for our digital solutions.

We would love the opportunity to meet you and discuss how our solutions can support your organization’s digital transformation. Please visit us at our booth to explore our platforms and experience the Latte Lab demonstration.
