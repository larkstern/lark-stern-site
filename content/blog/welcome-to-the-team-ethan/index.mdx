We’re excited to welcome **Ethan** to Lark & Stern as part of our software development team **🎉**

When he’s not coding, you’ll likely find him indulging in his greatest weakness — cheesecake — or diving into his love for cars and motorcycles. He also has a long-standing (and very committed) feud with Windows, choosing Arch Linux whenever he gets the chance. And if you ever see him smiling at his screen, it might just be because those tiny green squares on GitHub are adding up.

At 22 years old, he holds a **BSc in Computer Science** 🎓 **from the University of Pretoria** and brings a strong technical foundation to the team.

What excites him most about his role as a software developer is the opportunity to continuously learn — from new tools and technologies to better ways of building meaningful software. He’s especially looking forward to collaborating with other developers, learning from their different perspectives, and growing as part of a talented team. 🤝💡

We’re thrilled to have him on board and can’t wait to see what he builds with us.
