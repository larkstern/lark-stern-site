We all know what our teammates do, but how well do we really know the people behind the job titles?

We thought it was time to find out! We asked everyone on the team a few fun questions — from imaginary scenarios and personal choices to things they’d love to experience. The answers give us a little glimpse into the personalities, interests and imaginations that make our team who they are.💡

Scroll through and get to know your colleagues beyond the meetings, emails and work chats. You might discover a hidden talent, a shared interest, or an answer you definitely didn’t see coming!

## Here Are the Questions We Asked:💭

1. **If you could have any superpower, what would it be?**
2. **If you could master any skill instantly, what would you choose?**
3. **If you could live in any movie or TV series, which one would you choose?**
4. **If you could only eat one meal for a month, what would you choose?**
5. **If you could have any animal as a pet (realistic or not), what would you choose?**

Now, let’s see what our team had to say! 👀
