We’re excited to welcome **Ruby** to Lark & Stern **🎉**

Outside of work, she loves staying active — whether that’s going for a run or playing tennis with friends and family. She’s also a big movie lover and enjoys nothing more than watching a film in a movie theatre. Family plays a central role in her life, and she’s especially close with her siblings — fun fact: they’re all named after gemstones! 💎✨

In her role at Lark & Stern, she’s excited to use her skillset to make a positive contribution to the world, particularly within the pharmaceutical space. She’s looking forward to being mentally challenged, learning new concepts, and developing new skills along the way.

She’s confident that the year ahead will be one of immense growth and is eager to learn as much as possible from her amazing colleagues. We’re thrilled to have her on board and can’t wait to see the impact she’ll make.
