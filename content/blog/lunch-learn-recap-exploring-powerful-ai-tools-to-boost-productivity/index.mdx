##### Presented by: The Lark & Stern team

<Figure src="/blog/lunch-learn-recap-exploring-powerful-ai-tools-to-boost-productivity/image-1.jpg" />

In today’s fast-paced work environment, AI tools are more than just nice-to-haves — they’re becoming essential for working smarter, staying creative, and saving time. During our recent **Lunch & Learn**, a few team members shared their favorite AI platforms and how they’re using them to simplify tasks and elevate their output.

Here’s a quick recap of what we covered:

### **🎨 Gustav: Storydoc – Smarter Presentations Made Simple**

Gustav introduced us to **Storydoc**, a tool that transforms static slides into dynamic, interactive presentations — without the usual formatting headaches.

**✨ Highlights:**

- **Responsive Design** for any screen size
- **Engagement Analytics** to track viewer attention
- **Professional Templates** that are ready to use
- **Flexible Pricing** for individuals or teams

**💬 Why he loves it:** “Storydoc makes your message come alive — it’s a fantastic way to level up your presentations and keep your audience engaged.”

### **🎬 Frieda: Invideo AI – Effortless Video Creation with AI**

Frieda showcased **Invideo AI**, a platform that turns your ideas into videos — no video editing experience required.

**✨  Features:**

- **AI-Powered Video Generation**
- **Automated Script Writing**
- **Extensive Media Library**
- **Multilingual Capabilities**

**💬 Why it matters:** “Invideo AI streamlines video production from concept to final cut — perfect for marketing, training, or sharing knowledge visually.”

### **🧠 Jason: Napkin AI – Visual Thinking, Simplified**

Jason walked us through **Napkin AI**, a tool for structuring and visualizing thoughts in a more intuitive, connected way.

**✨  Key Features:**

- **Visual Brainstorming**
- **Smart Structuring** for complex ideas
- **Minimalist Interface** to avoid distractions

**💬 Why he uses it:** “It helps me untangle tricky ideas and get a clear picture of where I’m heading — perfect for planning or kicking off new projects.”

### **📄 Karla: NotebookLM – Smarter Document Analysis**

Karla introduced us to **NotebookLM**, a tool that helps you compare documents, pull out key insights, and generate summaries in seconds.

**✨  Features:**

- **Side-by-Side Document Comparison**
- **Insight Extraction**
- **Fast, Clear Summaries**

**💬 Why it’s useful:** “When you're juggling lots of files, NotebookLM helps you stay focused on what matters most — especially for researchers or content reviewers.”

### **🎧 Keean: Google NotebookLM – AI for Research & Content Creation**

Keean explored Google’s version of **NotebookLM**, highlighting its ability to interact with documents in smarter, more dynamic ways.

**✨  Features:**

- **Context-Aware Chatbot** to query your docs
- **Content Generation** for FAQs, guides, and more
- **Interactive Audio Clips** for podcast-style updates

**💬 Why it stands out:** “The podcast-style feature is a game-changer — it’s a creative way to share insights, team updates, or learning content.”

### **🧩 Final Thoughts**

This Lunch & Learn session was a great reminder of how AI tools are reshaping the way we work — making it easier to create, collaborate, and stay organized across roles.

**From storytelling and brainstorming to content creation and research, AI is unlocking new levels of productivity.**
