The recent SAPHEX Expo, held on 25 & 26 March 2026, hosted at the Sandton Convention Centre, once again proved why it remains a standout event in the pharmaceutical industry calendar.

From the very first day, the energy on the expo floor was undeniable. With a busy and well-organized start, the event created the perfect environment for meaningful engagement, industry insights, and valuable connections.

For Lark & Stern, this year's focus was simple yet impactful: connecting with clients and showcasing how we support the industry through innovative solutions and equipment integration. Our stand, the Latte Lab, became a central talking point throughout the event, offering a unique and engaging way to present our services, particularly around Simplified Manufacturing Process Layer (SiMPL).

The Latte Lab not only drew attention but also created a welcoming space for conversation and engagement. It allowed our team to engage with potential clients, demonstrate our solutions in a relaxed yet professional environment, and create meaningful face-to-face interactions. This interactive approach helped set our presence apart at the expo.

One of the key highlights of SAPHEX 2026 was the opportunity to interact directly with a wide range of industry professionals. These conversations reinforced the importance of collaboration within the pharmaceutical sector and highlighted the growing demand for streamlined, efficient digital solutions.

Beyond business engagement, the expo was also a valuable team experience. With the entire Lark & Stern team in attendance, it provided an opportunity to work closely together in a dynamic environment, strengthen team collaboration, and represent the brand with energy, unity, and professionalism.

Overall, SAPHEX 2026 delivered a well-rounded experience that was professional, engaging, and enjoyable. It was not only a platform to showcase what we do on a small scale, but also a reminder of the strong relationships and collaborations that continue to drive the industry forward.

We would like to thank everyone who visited our stand and took the time to connect with us. If you didn't get the chance to meet the team, we would love to continue the conversation. Let's connect and explore how Lark & Stern can support your journey.
